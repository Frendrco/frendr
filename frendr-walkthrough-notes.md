# Frendr Walkthrough Video — Script & Shot List

## Suggested Flow (8–12 min)

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:30)
- Open on the landing page — scroll through the discover grid
- One line: *"This is Frendr — a place for motion designers to share work, find inspiration, and connect with people who actually get it."*
- Don't over-explain. Let the work on screen do the talking.

### 2. Signing Up (0:30–1:30)
- Click Join Free → Clerk sign-up flow
- Onboarding: name, username, pick skills
- Land on the discover feed — first impression moment

### 3. Your Profile (1:30–3:00)
- Navigate to your profile page
- Show the avatar, display name, role, pronouns, creator type badge
- Point out Available for Work toggle
- Skills pills, bio, social links
- Edit profile modal — show how quick it is to update
- Cover image upload

### 4. Uploading Work (3:00–5:30)
- Hit Upload — show the three types:
  - **Portfolio** — finished client work, full metadata, tags, privacy controls
  - **Recess** — quick experiments, tool tests, no polish required. Show the tool badges (Blender, After Effects, Rive, etc.)
  - **Interactive** — Rive work, let people play with it
- Walk through the upload flow: drop file → thumbnail → basics → privacy → embed settings (autoplay, loop)
- Show the frame picker for thumbnails

### 5. The Video Page (5:30–7:00)
- Open a published video
- Cloudflare Stream player — point out autoplay/loop if set
- Action bar: like, upvote, save to playlist, add to channel, download, share
- Creator sidebar — follow button, their other work
- Tags → clicking one filters discover

### 6. Discovery & Search (7:00–8:30)
- Back to home — category filter pills (Motion Design, 2D, 3D, VFX, etc.)
- Trending vs Newest toggle
- Recess row — quick scrollable experiments from the community
- Search — type a keyword, show results across videos, creators, channels

### 7. Channels (8:30–9:30)
- Open a channel — curated collection with a colour identity
- Show the Add to Channel button on a video card (hover)
- Create a channel — 3-step wizard, pick a colour

### 8. Playlists & Watch Later (9:30–10:15)
- Hover a video card → bookmark icon → Save to playlist
- Watch Later queue
- Your playlists on your profile

### 9. Community (10:15–11:00)
- Community tab — discussion threads
- Post a thread: text, bold/italic, links
- Vote on threads, reply

### 10. Close (11:00–end)
- Back to discover grid — scroll through beautiful work
- *"Frendr is built for people who make things move. Sign up free at frendr.co"*

---

## Things to Emphasise
- Speed — everything feels fast, no friction
- The three video types and what makes each one different
- Recess as a low-stakes place to share experiments (not just polished work)
- The community isn't generic social media — it's people who care about craft

## Things to Avoid Saying
- "Innovative platform" / "revolutionary"
- Long feature lists with no demo
- Lingering on sign-up/auth screens

## Shot Tips
- Use a real account with real videos already uploaded
- Have a second account ready to demo Follow / messaging
- Record at 2x retina if on Mac — looks sharp on any screen
- Trim dead air aggressively — keep energy up
